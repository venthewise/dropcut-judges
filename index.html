One shot per photo, in order. Jpg, png, or webp, 25 MB each. 5, 10 and 15 s reels take exactly 5 photos. 30 s reels take 5 to 10 photos. 60 s reels take exactly 10 photos. The song must run at least the reel length from your start point, and it is trimmed to that length with a short fade-out. Your own song can be up to 20 MB.

STEP 2Pick a look and write a caption for each shot. Rearrange the images as needed by dragging them or by using the arrow buttons.

02

How it works

Three plates. One cut.
  1. PLATE 01

    Photos + song

    Up to five stills and a track. That is the brief. No moodboard committee.

    ABCWAV
  2. PLATE 02

    Beats become cuts

    The waveform is a ruler. Bars are registration marks. Every cut lands on a downbeat.

  3. PLATE 03

    Reel out

    Each still becomes a moving shot via LTX image-to-video, then hard-cut, captioned and muxed with the song.
